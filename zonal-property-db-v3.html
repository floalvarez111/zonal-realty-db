How image extraction works

1.Upload any developer image — pricelist table, brochure, or Messenger screenshot.
2.Claude reads the image and extracts TCP, reservation fee, monthly amortization, DP terms, loanable amount, and loan term.
3.Review the extracted figures, then hit "Apply to form fields" — all pricing fields fill automatically.
4.Manually correct anything the AI missed. You always have final say on the numbers.
